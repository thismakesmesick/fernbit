Hi! I’m Sophia.

Tbh, I don’t really like writing about myself like this. I also don’t think it matters that much who I am. I have no interest in branding myself or forcing a niche¹.

This blog isn’t really about me anyway. It’s about writing. Communicating. Without cutting things down or censoring them.

I want to say things fully — and I want you to too. I want long, run-on essays about what we actually think, not the version that survives the algorithm.

I miss my netizens. The people who made their own homes on the internet. I don’t want to be spoon-fed ads and aesthetics — I want to find my people.

Let’s revive the internet!

¹ /niːʃ/ not /nɪtʃ/
